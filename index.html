About Naturals Organic

Pure since 2019

It started with one beehive in a family orchard in Tangail. What we found — honey that actually tasted like the wildflowers it came from — was rare enough that friends kept asking where to buy more.

Today, Naturals Organic partners with over 40 smallholder farms across Bangladesh, from honey collectors in the Sundarbans to nut growers in the north. Every jar is lab-tested, every oil is cold-pressed, and nothing ever gets diluted, bleached, or rushed.

We deliver to all 64 districts — because good food shouldn't only reach the capital.
